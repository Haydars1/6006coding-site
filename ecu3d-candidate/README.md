# ECU 3D candidate acceptance

The production homepage remains on the existing real three-connector ECU photographs. Do not promote the rejected procedural models from PR #7, #9 or #10. A new model requires independently movable cover and PCB, three correctly shaped connectors, commercial-use rights evidence, and six mobile screenshots (three stages at 390x844 and 430x932) reviewed before publication.

Research on 2026-10-11 found no free, commercially licensed, multi-angle exact-match three-socket ECU CAD/GLB suitable for the site's reference. A free generic low-poly ECU model is not an acceptable replacement. Do not fabricate unseen geometry or claim a photo sequence is true 3D.

Source candidates to investigate (not yet approved):
- https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library (CC0 parts library, no exact match confirmed)
- https://www.cgtrader.com/free-3d-models/car/car/basic-ecu (free generic low-poly, not suitable)
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EDC17CP20.jpg (reference photography; licensing and model matching must be checked)

Do not change `dist/index.html`, Render deployment, or the existing photo preview until the exact model is independently verified.
